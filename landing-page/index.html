Decoding the Signals

Hover over the waveform to understand the electrical language of your heart.

P-Wave: The Atrial Spark

Represents the electrical activation (depolarization) of the atria—the upper chambers.

⚠️ Hazards if Abnormal

  • Atrial Fibrillation: Missing or chaotic P-waves. Increases stroke risk by 5x.
  • Heart Block: Disconnected from QRS. Signals aren't reaching ventricles.

QRS Complex: The Power Pump

The massive electrical discharge causing the ventricles (lower chambers) to contract and pump blood to the body.

⚠️ Hazards if Abnormal

  • Wide QRS: Can indicate Bundle Branch Block or Ventricular Tachycardia (Cardiac Arrest risk).
  • High Amplitude: Sign of Ventricular Hypertrophy (enlarged heart).

T-Wave: The Reset

The recovery phase (repolarization) where the heart recharges for the next beat.

⚠️ Hazards if Abnormal

  • Inverted T: Classic sign of Ischemia (Heart Attack in progress).
  • Peaked T: Hyperkalemia (High Potassium) - dangerous for rhythms.

The Silent Enemy: Plaque & Inflammation

See how arteries clog over time due to inflammation and poor diet.

🔥 What is Inflammation?

Chronic inflammation damages the inner lining of arteries. Your body generates "plaque" (cholesterol, calcium, fat) to patch this damage, but this patch narrows the road.

🍔 The Diet Connection

Processed foods, trans fats, and excess sugar trigger this inflammation.

Avoid: Deep fried food, High Fructose Corn Syrup
Eat: Omega-3 (Salmon), Berries (Antioxidants), Leafy Greens

In Case of Emergency

Recognize the signs. Act fast. Time is muscle.

1. Recognize

Chest pressure/pain, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea.

2. Call

Dial Emergency Services immediately. Do not drive yourself.

3. Aspirin

Chew 325mg Aspirin (if not allergic) to slow clot formation.
